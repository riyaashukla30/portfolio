Last-Mile Delivery Tracker

Aug 2026 · full-stack, built in phases

The problem

Deliveries fail, riders are far away, and customers get no updates. Someone has to assign, price and reschedule all of it.

What I built

A platform for three roles (Customer, Agent, Admin) with live order tracking, automatic assignment of the nearest agent, and a flow for failed deliveries and reschedules.

The impact

An order goes from placed to assigned without a human in the middle, and customers are told by email and SMS when something goes wrong.

the interesting bitReal deliveries don't always go to plan. Instead of treating a failed delivery as an error, I made it a proper flow: the customer is told by email and SMS, the order can be rescheduled, and the story carries on.

What makes it stand out
  • Haversine-distance auto-assignment of the closest agent
  • A configurable rate engine with 4 zone-based rate cards
  • JWT auth with role-based access, and Jest/Supertest tests on auth, pricing and the reschedule flow
  • Deployed on Vercel and Render

RAG Chatbot

Jul 2026 · internship capstone

The problem

Finding one answer inside a pile of documents means opening them one by one and searching manually.

What I built

A chatbot you point at your own documents. It indexes them, finds the relevant passages for each question, and hands them to an LLM to answer.

The impact

Direct answers grounded in your own files instead of generic replies, which was the point of making it retrieval-based.

the interesting bitA chatbot that guesses is easy to build. The harder goal was making it answer from your own documents, so every reply starts with retrieval instead of imagination.

What makes it stand out
  • Full ingestion pipeline: load, chunk, embed, index in FAISS
  • LangChain retrieval wired to an LLM for grounded responses
